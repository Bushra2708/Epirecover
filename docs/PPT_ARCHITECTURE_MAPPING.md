# EpiRecover — PPT Architecture Mapping

**Project**: EpiRecover: Evidence-Grounded Multimodal Reconstruction of Damaged Ancient Indian Inscriptions from Monument Imagery  
**Primary Specification**: `EpiRecover_Project_Work_I.pptx` (Batch No: 1, Dept. of AI & Data Science)  
**Scope**: Tamil-Language Inscriptions Only (Modern Tamil & Tamil-Brahmi scripts)  
**Constraint**: Strictly Non-Generative (No Generative LLMs; Evidence-Grounded Reconstruction Only)
